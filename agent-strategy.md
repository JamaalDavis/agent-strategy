# Agent Strategy & Leadership Plan
**Inclusive Tech Club | Q3 2026**

---

## 1. My Business Priority

**Grow club membership and visibility by getting our message in front of more people, consistently.**

This quarter: double our reach — more people knowing we exist, what we stand for, and why they belong here. The bottleneck is not mission clarity; it's bandwidth. Club leadership is stretched thin. Agents handle the repeatable communication work so officers can focus on community and relationships.

---

## 2. What Stays With Me

These never go to an agent:

- **Community voice and values.** What "inclusive" means for our club — who we center, what language we use, what we won't amplify. Agents can draft; leadership decides if it reflects us.
- **Member and speaker relationships.** 1:1 conversations, welcoming new members, following up after events. No automated message goes to a real person without human review.
- **Strategic direction.** Which partnerships to pursue, which events to prioritize, which communities to build bridges with.
- **Accountability.** Anything that goes out under the club's name is the officers' responsibility — not the model's.

---

## 3. What the Agents Do

### Research & CRM Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Keep a clean, up-to-date record of members, prospective speakers, and partner organizations |
| **Input** | Event sign-up forms, newsletter signups, speaker inquiry emails |
| **Output** | Enriched contact records tagged by interest area, affiliation, and engagement history |
| **Human checkpoint** | Officer reviews new contacts weekly before any outreach is triggered |

---

### Content Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Draft announcements, social posts, and newsletters from a brief so officers start at 60%, not 0% |
| **Input** | A short brief: event or topic, target audience (students, faculty, industry), channel (Instagram, email, LinkedIn), and 2–3 examples of past content we liked |
| **Output** | A ready-to-edit draft with headline, body, and a call to action formatted for the channel |
| **Human checkpoint** | Communications officer reads every draft, rewrites anything that doesn't sound like us, approves before scheduling — nothing posts automatically |

---

### Outbound Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Research and build a shortlist of potential speakers, sponsors, or partner clubs worth reaching out to |
| **Input** | A target profile: e.g., "Black women in ML at mid-size tech companies" or "student orgs focused on accessibility" |
| **Output** | A ranked list of 10–15 candidates with names, affiliations, public contact info, and a one-line personalization note for each |
| **Human checkpoint** | Leadership reviews the list, removes anyone who isn't a fit, writes or approves the actual outreach message before anything is sent |

---

## 4. How I Govern It

### Free to run (reversible, low-stakes)
- Generating draft posts or email copy
- Researching potential speakers or partners
- Tagging and organizing contact records
- Reformatting content for a different channel

### Requires human first (anything outward-facing or irreversible)
- Any message sent to a member, speaker, or external org
- Any post published under the club's accounts
- Any outreach that represents the club to a new contact
- Any content touching identity, community, or sensitive topics — always a human reads it first

### Roles

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Runs the agent, writes the brief, first reader of output (e.g., Communications Officer) |
| **Reviewer** | Checks voice, values alignment, and accuracy before anything goes out (e.g., Club President or VP) |
| **Approver** | Final sign-off on anything sent to external contacts or published publicly |

### Review Rhythm

| Cadence | What you check |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Any drafts in the queue; flag anything that missed the mark on tone or inclusion |
| **Weekly** | Content published vs. target; new contacts added to CRM; outreach shortlist reviewed |
| **Monthly** | Is membership actually growing? Are agents saving officer time? Adjust briefs or process where the bottleneck shifted |

---

**One-line summary:** The agents handle research, drafts, and prospecting. Club leadership owns the voice, the relationships, and every message that goes out under our name.
