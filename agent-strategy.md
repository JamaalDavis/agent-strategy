# Agent Strategy & Leadership Plan
**ICT (Inclusive Tech Club) | Q3 2026**

---

## 1. ICT's Priority This Quarter

**Grow ICT membership and visibility by getting our message in front of more people, consistently.**

This quarter: double ICT's reach — more students knowing we exist, what we stand for, and why they belong here. The bottleneck is not mission clarity; it's bandwidth. ICT officers are stretched thin across events, academics, and community building. Agents handle the repeatable communication work so ICT leadership can focus on the people and relationships that make this club real.

---

## 2. What Stays With ICT Leadership

These never go to an agent:

- **ICT's voice and values.** What "inclusive" means for this club — who we center, what language we use, what we won't amplify. Agents can draft; ICT leadership decides if it sounds like us.
- **Member and speaker relationships.** 1:1 conversations, welcoming new members, following up after ICT events. No automated message goes to a real person without human review.
- **Strategic direction.** Which partnerships ICT pursues, which events we prioritize, which communities we build bridges with.
- **Accountability.** Anything that goes out under ICT's name is the officers' responsibility — not the model's.

---

## 3. What the Agents Do for ICT

### Research & CRM Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Keep a clean, up-to-date record of ICT members, prospective speakers, and partner organizations |
| **Input** | ICT event sign-up forms, newsletter signups, speaker inquiry emails |
| **Output** | Enriched contact records tagged by interest area (accessibility, ML, cybersecurity, etc.), school affiliation, and ICT engagement history |
| **Human checkpoint** | ICT officer reviews new contacts weekly before any outreach is triggered |

---

### Content Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Draft ICT announcements, social posts, and newsletters from a brief so officers start at 60%, not 0% |
| **Input** | A short brief: ICT event or topic, target audience (students, faculty, industry allies), channel (Instagram, email, LinkedIn), and 2–3 examples of past ICT content we liked |
| **Output** | A ready-to-edit draft with headline, body, and a call to action formatted for the channel |
| **Human checkpoint** | ICT Communications Officer reads every draft, rewrites anything that doesn't sound like us, approves before scheduling — nothing posts automatically |

---

### Outbound Agent

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Single job** | Research and build a shortlist of potential speakers, sponsors, or partner clubs aligned with ICT's mission |
| **Input** | A target profile: e.g., "Black women engineers at mid-size tech companies" or "student orgs focused on accessibility and disability inclusion" |
| **Output** | A ranked list of 10–15 candidates with names, affiliations, public contact info, and a one-line personalization note connecting them to ICT's work |
| **Human checkpoint** | ICT leadership reviews the list, removes anyone who isn't a fit, writes or approves the actual outreach message before anything is sent |

---

## 4. How ICT Governs It

### Free to run (reversible, low-stakes)
- Generating draft posts or email copy for ICT events
- Researching potential speakers or partner orgs
- Tagging and organizing ICT member records
- Reformatting one piece of content across channels (e.g., event recap → Instagram caption + email blurb)

### Requires human first (anything outward-facing or irreversible)
- Any message sent to an ICT member, speaker, or external org
- Any post published under ICT's social accounts
- Any outreach that represents ICT to a new contact
- Any content touching identity, community, or sensitive topics — an ICT officer always reads it first

### Roles

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Runs the agent, writes the brief, first reader of output (ICT Communications Officer) |
| **Reviewer** | Checks voice, values alignment, and accuracy before anything goes out (ICT President or VP) |
| **Approver** | Final sign-off on anything sent to external contacts or published publicly |

### Review Rhythm

| Cadence | What ICT checks |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Drafts in the queue; flag anything that missed the mark on tone or ICT values |
| **Weekly** | Content published vs. target; new contacts added to CRM; outreach shortlist reviewed |
| **Monthly** | Is ICT membership actually growing? Are agents saving officer time? Adjust briefs or process where the bottleneck shifted |

---

**One-line summary:** The agents handle research, drafts, and prospecting. ICT leadership owns the voice, the relationships, and every message that goes out under our name.
